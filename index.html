📖 How to Play

  • 🔴 Players take turns dropping discs into any column.
  • ⬇️ The disc automatically falls to the lowest empty slot.
  • 🎯 Connect 4 discs horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
  • 🏆 The first player to connect 4 discs wins.
  • 🤝 If the board fills up with no winner, it's a draw.
  • ↩️ Use Undo to revert your last move.
  • 🤖 In AI mode, try to outsmart the computer.
  • 🎨 In PvP mode, customize disc colors with presets!
